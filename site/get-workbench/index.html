Live Workbench

Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Opera can connect to Opalinx controllers through Web Serial. Safari can open and explore the interface, but cannot connect to a serial device.

Portable builds include their own browser runtime and support direct controller connections without relying on the browser's Web Serial implementation.
